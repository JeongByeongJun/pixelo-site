How it works

Call, point, hand it off

⌘+E
Step 1

Call Pixelo

Press ⌘E in PowerPoint, Excel, Word or your browser. Pixelo hops next to your cursor, dressed for that app.

Step 2

Point and talk

Circle it, tap it or draw a path, then say what you want with ⌥. Each mark gets a number, so “this” and “there” stay paired with your words.

"Design"slide 2B4:E7paragraph 3word "revenue"[330, 150]
Step 3

Hand it off

Press ↵. Your marks become shape names, cell ranges, words and coordinates in your AI’s chat. Its answer shows up as a card right where you pointed.

Quick start

Running in three commands

  • macOS with Accessibility permission for your terminal
  • Node.js 23.6+
  • Claude Code or another AI chat that speaks MCP
  • Optional: an ElevenLabs key for voice
From source 
$git clone https://github.com/jhl72e/Pixelo.git
$cd Pixelo && npm install && npm run dev
$claude mcp add --transport http pixelo http://localhost:9340/mcp

FAQ

Questions

What is Pixelo?

A pointing companion for your AI. It sits next to your cursor, turns your circles, taps and paths into the names and coordinates your app uses, and drops them into your AI’s chat together with what you said.

Does Pixelo edit my files?

No. Your AI does the editing with its own tools, such as AppleScript for Office. Pixelo only reads the screen and tells your AI what you pointed at, so you can undo every change in the app as usual.

Which AI does it work with?

Claude Code is the main path, connected through MCP. Pixelo can also type into the Claude desktop app and the Claude panel in PowerPoint.

What leaves my Mac?

Your marks and a snapshot of the marked screen go to the AI chat you picked. Voice uses ElevenLabs with your own key, only while you are talking. Nothing else is uploaded.

Does it cost anything?

Pixelo is free and open source. You only pay for the AI you already use.
